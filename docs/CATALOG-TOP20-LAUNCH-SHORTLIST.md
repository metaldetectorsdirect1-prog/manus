## Top 5 hero products

### 1. Double Breasted Tailored Blazer
- **Landed** $20.90 · **Retail** $79 · **Gross** $58.10 (73.5%)
- **Why it can win** — the only US-warehouse product in the batch with a
  GREEN multiple *and* a $58 gross. 13-day delivery is credible for paid
  traffic; blazers are evergreen rather than a six-week seasonal window.
- **Angle** — "the blazer that does workwear and going-out", styled two
  ways. Sell the silhouette, not the fabric (fibre is unverified).
- **Best channel** — Google Shopping / PMax first; the query volume for
  "double breasted blazer women" is transactional and the image is clean
  enough to compete on a grid.
- **Biggest risk** — fit complaints. No measurements are verified, and
  blazers are the highest-return-rate item in this batch. Get a size chart
  from the supplier before activating.

### 2. Timeless Tailored Wool Blend Lapel Coat
- **Landed** $31.00 · **Retail** $119 · **Gross** $88.00 (73.9%)
- **Why it can win** — highest gross dollars of any Tier A product, and
  it lands in the exact seasonal window the playbook calls for (import at
  the trough in late August for the Oct–Feb peak).
- **Angle** — the winter-coat purchase as a considered buy: one clean
  hero shot, one on-body, one detail. Premium presentation earns $119.
- **Best channel** — Google Shopping, then Meta retargeting. High AOV
  supports a higher CPA than anything else here.
- **Biggest risk** — "wool blend" is the supplier's word. Do not publish a
  wool percentage without a spec sheet, or it becomes a claims problem.

### 3. Structured Everyday Shoulder Tote
- **Landed** $24.46 · **Retail** $79 · **Gross** $54.54 (69.0%)
- **Why it can win** — the only bag that survived the trademark screen
  this pass, and it survived precisely because it has no logo, no
  monogram and no hardware branding. Free shipping, 12 days.
- **Angle** — capacity and structure ("holds a 13-inch laptop" — measure
  first), shot flat and on-shoulder. Q4 gifting demand.
- **Best channel** — Meta and Pinterest; bags are visually driven and
  bags convert on aesthetic rather than search intent.
- **Biggest risk** — PU leather durability complaints, and dimensions are
  unverified. Never describe it as leather.

### 4. Luxe Satin V-Neck Slip Maxi Dress
- **Landed** $21.15 · **Retail** $76 · **Gross** $54.85 (72.2%)
- **Why it can win** — the strongest external demand evidence in the
  batch: Meshki (+450K 7-day reach) and Ninepine (+338K) are both scaling
  slip-dress creative into the US right now, and Q4 occasion-wear demand
  is ahead of us, not behind.
- **Angle** — occasion dressing at a non-occasion price.
- **Best channel** — Meta first (the category is proven there by the
  advertisers above), Google Shopping second.
- **Biggest risk** — colour fidelity on satin, and returns on fit. Also
  the lead image is a supplier boutique photo whose provenance cannot be
  verified from here — worth re-shooting or background-standardising
  before scale.

### 5. Vintage Washed Baggy Wide-Leg Jeans
- **Landed** $20.43 · **Retail** $71 · **Gross** $50.57 (70.7%)
- **Why it can win** — wide-leg denim carries the single largest reach
  signal captured in research (Ninepine's wide pant, +523K 7-day), and
  this is the one denim listing in the batch that clears the floor on
  free shipping.
- **Angle** — the fit ("baggy but not shapeless"), shot full-length.
- **Best channel** — TikTok and Meta; denim fit content performs as
  organic-style creative.
- **Biggest risk** — denim sizing is the highest-variance fit category
  there is. A real measurement table is a precondition, not a nice-to-have.
