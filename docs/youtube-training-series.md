# HIVOLT men's training series — production package

Built 2026-08-14. Channel `UCNKiN3BUowGAIetc7--hqsg`. Format is vertical
9:16 Shorts, because that is the format the comparables actually won on
(`audit/ORGANIC-WINNERS-2026-08-12.md`: post count predicts traffic, followers
do not; winners posted 227–1,523 times, HIVOLT has posted 12) and because the
10M-Shorts-views route is the realistic path into YPP for a channel starting
from zero.

## Three constraints this package is built around

**1. Nothing here depicts a HIVOLT garment.** The standing rule is that
synthetic images of garments a customer receives are misrepresentation. Every
prompt specifies plain, unbranded, logo-free kit, and no shot is framed as a
product shot — no fabric close-ups, no "shop this look", no g/m² claim on
screen. The videos sell the brand's competence, not a SKU whose appearance
would be fabricated. Product photography stays real supplier imagery.

**2. Volume is the enemy here, not the goal.** YouTube's inauthentic-content
policy makes mass-produced, repetitious content ineligible for the Partner
Programme. YPP is the gate on YouTube Shopping, which is the only thing that
connects this channel to the store. Uploading forty near-identical AI clips is
the fastest way to lose the thing the channel is being built for. Each entry
below is a distinct movement, a distinct error, and a distinct script.

**3. The form cues are written here, not generated.** A video model
choreographs; it does not coach, and it will happily render a rep that would
hurt someone. Every cue below is a standard, conservative coaching point for a
well-established compound movement. The video shows the movement; the cues
carry the instruction, as on-screen text and description. Nothing prescribes
load, volume, or rehabilitation.

## The eight

Each is one movement, one common error, one fix. Ordered by search demand
against production difficulty.

### 01 — Romanian deadlift *(pilot, produced)*

**Hook (0:00–0:02):** "Your RDL is a squat and you don't know it."
**On-screen text:** `HINGE, NOT SQUAT` → `PUSH HIPS BACK` → `BAR STAYS ON THE LEG`
**The error:** bending the knees and dropping the hips, turning a hip hinge
into a shallow squat, so the hamstrings never load.
**The cues:**
- Soft knees, then hold that angle. The knee does not travel further.
- Push the hips back toward the wall behind you. That is the whole movement.
- The bar stays in contact with the thigh the entire descent.
- Stop when the hamstrings stop lengthening — for most people that is
  mid-shin, not the floor.
- Ribs down, shoulders back, neutral spine throughout.

**Thumbnail:** athlete at the bottom of the hinge, hard cold rim light on the
hamstring line, arrow to the hip crease, three stacked words `RDL / FIXED /
IN 10 SECONDS`.

**Description:**
> The Romanian deadlift trains the hip hinge, not the knee. If your knees
> travel forward on the way down you have turned it into a squat and the
> hamstrings never take the load. Soft knees, hold the angle, push the hips
> back, keep the bar on the leg. Stop where the stretch stops.
>
> HIVOLT publishes the fabric weight of every garment it sells in g/m².
> hivolt-usa.com

### 02 — Bench press, elbow path

**Hook:** "Flared elbows are why your shoulder clicks."
**On-screen text:** `45° NOT 90°` → `TUCK ON THE WAY DOWN`
**The error:** elbows flaring to ninety degrees at the bottom, loading the
front of the shoulder.
**The cues:** elbows tuck toward roughly 45° from the torso; bar meets the
lower chest, not the collarbone; wrists stacked over the elbows; shoulder
blades pulled back and down and held there; feet planted.

Cross-links to the existing blog article on shoulder-friendly pressing.

---

### 03 — Barbell row, torso angle

**Hook:** "If you're standing up, you're not rowing."
**On-screen text:** `45° TORSO` → `PULL TO THE HIP` → `NO BODY ENGLISH`
**The cues:** hinge to roughly 45°; pull toward the bottom of the ribcage or
the hip, not the chest; elbows drive back past the ribs; the torso angle does
not change during the rep; if it does, the load is too heavy.

---

### 04 — Pull-up, the dead hang

**Hook:** "Half a rep counts as zero."
**On-screen text:** `FULL HANG` → `CHEST TO BAR` → `NO KIP`
**The cues:** start from a full dead hang with the elbows straight; pull the
elbows down and back rather than the chin up; chest toward the bar; lower
under control for a count of two; no swing.

---

### 05 — Overhead press, ribs

**Hook:** "Your lower back is doing your shoulders' job."
**On-screen text:** `RIBS DOWN` → `SQUEEZE THE GLUTES` → `HEAD THROUGH AT THE TOP`
**The cues:** brace the midsection and keep the ribcage down — the arch is the
lower back compensating for shoulder mobility; glutes tight; bar path close to
the face; at lockout the head moves through, so the bar finishes over the
mid-foot.

---

### 06 — Back squat, depth versus butt wink

**Hook:** "Deeper isn't better if your pelvis tucks."
**On-screen text:** `FIND YOUR DEPTH` → `STOP AT THE TUCK`
**The cues:** descend until the pelvis begins to tuck under, then stop — that
is your depth today; knees track over the toes; whole foot stays down;
stance width is individual, so widen until the bottom position feels stable.

---

### 07 — Face pulls

**Hook:** "The 90 seconds that keep you pressing."
**On-screen text:** `PULL TO THE FOREHEAD` → `THUMBS BACK` → `LIGHT AND SLOW`
**The cues:** rope at roughly eye height; pull toward the forehead; finish with
the thumbs pointing behind you; light load, controlled, high reps; this is
positional work, not a strength lift.

---

### 08 — Hip flexor mobility for desk workers

**Hook:** "Sitting all day is why you can't lock out."
**On-screen text:** `TUCK THE PELVIS` → `SQUEEZE THE BACK GLUTE` → `30s EACH SIDE`
**The cues:** half-kneeling; tuck the pelvis under before moving forward;
squeeze the glute on the kneeling side; the stretch should appear at the front
of the hip, not in the lower back; do not push into pain.

## The Seedance 2.5 prompt pattern

What produced a usable pilot, and worth keeping constant so the eight read as
one series rather than eight unrelated clips:

- **Subject:** "lean, athletic man in his early thirties", one person, one
  movement, described as a sequence of positions rather than as an action word
- **Wardrobe, always explicit:** "plain unbranded charcoal training shorts and
  a plain black fitted t-shirt, no logos, no text, no graphics on the clothing"
- **Camera:** low three-quarter angle, slow push-in with a slight arc
- **Light:** hard directional key from camera left, deep shadow falloff, one
  cold blue practical in the background
- **Grade:** cool steel-grey, near-black, single blue accent
- **Environment:** dark industrial gym, matte black rubber flooring, chalk dust
- **Always append:** "no on-screen text, no captions, no watermarks" — burnt-in
  model text cannot be edited and will be wrong

Settings: `seedance_2_5`, `mode: t2v`, `duration: 10`, `resolution: 1080p`,
`aspect_ratio: 9:16`, `generate_audio: true`. **90 credits each.**

The prompt trips a preset recommendation ("IN THE DARK"); pass
`declined_preset_id` to render the prompt literally.

## Thumbnails

Shorts are served from the first frame, so thumbnails matter for the 16:9
cuts, not the vertical ones. Model `nano_banana_pro`, 16:9.

Rules that survive being 120px wide in a sidebar: three words maximum, stacked;
one accent colour and no second; the athlete occupies two thirds and the text
one third; an arrow pointing at the body part the video is about; no HIVOLT
logo, because a logo at thumbnail size is illegible and spends the space that
should carry the promise.

## What this package does not do

**It does not upload.** `youtube.com` and `studio.youtube.com` both fail at the
proxy's CONNECT step — measured, not assumed — and no YouTube OAuth is held
here. Files get produced; a person publishes them.

**It does not claim these will trend.** "Trending" was scoped from search
demand for the movements and from the comparables' own posting behaviour, not
from live trend data — `www.googleapis.com` is reachable but needs an API key,
and no YouTube Data API key is configured.

**One video is produced, not eight.** 631 credits buys seven at this spec.
Spending six of them before anyone has judged whether the first is good enough
to carry a brand would be the wrong order.
