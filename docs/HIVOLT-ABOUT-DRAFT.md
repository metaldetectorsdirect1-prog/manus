## About HIVOLT

**Who we are.**
HIVOLT is a womenswear label operated by Dn Global Trading LLC, based in
Willowbrook, Illinois. We sell in the United States, with free tracked
shipping on every order.

**What we sell.**
Women's clothing, edited deliberately — a catalog that grows piece by
piece, starting with knitwear. We would rather list fewer garments we can
stand behind than a thousand we cannot.

**How we describe products.**
We publish what we can verify and leave out what we cannot. When a
composition, a measurement, or a care instruction appears on a product
page, it comes from the garment's own documentation — never from a guess.
If a detail is missing, that's because we won't invent it.

**Our promise on every order.**
Free tracked US shipping, no minimum. Sixty days from delivery to return
anything, for any reason, with a prepaid label and no restocking fee.
Checkout runs on Shopify's secure checkout.
