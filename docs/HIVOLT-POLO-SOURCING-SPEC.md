# HIVOLT — Polo Sourcing Specification

The product HIVOLT **should source**, not whatever a marketplace happens to
offer. This is the brief to send to manufacturers.

> Values marked `[OWNER]` are commercial decisions. Values marked `[QUOTE]` come
> back from the supplier. Nothing here is invented — unknowns stay unknown.

## MUST HAVE — disqualifying if absent

| # | Requirement | Target / acceptance | Why it is non-negotiable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Published fabric composition** | Exact % split, on a spec sheet | §5 forbids claims not backed by product data. No spec = no marketing story |
| 2 | **Published GSM** | 180–240 gsm for a year-round premium polo | The single number that separates "premium" from "cheap" in customer perception and in ad copy |
| 3 | **True US/EU sizing** | Garment measurements per size, US-standard | Weight-based Asian sizing is the #1 avoidable return driver |
| 4 | **Size tolerance** | ±1 cm on chest/length | Inconsistent sizing destroys repeat purchase |
| 5 | **Shrinkage tested** | ≤3% after 3 home washes, documented | A polo that shrinks is a refund and a lost customer |
| 6 | **Colour consistency across production runs** | ΔE ≤ 1.5 between lots | Reorders must match the photography and prior purchases |
| 7 | **Private neck label** | Woven or printed HIVOLT label | Without it this is not a brand, it is a reseller |
| 8 | **Defect allowance defined** | ≤2%, with remedy terms | Otherwise QC cost is unbounded |
| 9 | **Reorder lead time** | ≤6 weeks | Longer breaks the ability to scale advertising into stock |

## STRONGLY PREFERRED

| # | Requirement | Target | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | Pilling resistance | ICI ≥ 4 after 5,000 rubs | Directly supports a durability claim |
| 11 | Stretch + recovery | 2–4% elastane; ≥95% recovery | Enables "stretch" claim honestly |
| 12 | Moisture management | Wicking test documented | **Only** basis on which performance positioning becomes legal |
| 13 | Wrinkle resistance | Documented finish | Travel/work angle |
| 14 | Collar construction | Ribbed self-fabric, fused or tipped; must not curl after wash | The single most visible quality tell on a polo |
| 15 | Placket construction | Clean-finished, reinforced, 2–3 button | Second most visible tell |
| 16 | Button quality | Mother-of-pearl or high-grade resin, cross-stitched | Cheap buttons undo a premium impression instantly |
| 17 | Stitching | ≥12 stitches/inch; side-seamed, not tubular | Side seams read as tailored |
| 18 | Embroidery compatibility | Confirmed for logo placement | HIVOLT mark on chest/sleeve |
| 19 | Hang tags + packaging | Branded, recyclable | Unboxing is retention |

## Commercial terms to obtain `[QUOTE]`

MOQ per style · MOQ per colourway · sample cost · sampling lead time ·
production lead time · reorder lead time · per-unit cost at 300/500/1,000/2,500
· branding cost (label, tag, embroidery) · packaging cost · freight (air/sea) ·
duties by destination · **landed cost USA / Canada / UK / EU** · payment terms ·
defect remedy · fulfilment compatibility (can they ship to a 3PL, or blind-ship).

## Sampling protocol before any commitment

1. Order samples in **3 sizes** (S, L, XXL) from each shortlisted supplier.
2. Measure every sample against the spec sheet — record deviation.
3. Wash 3× at home settings; re-measure for shrinkage; inspect collar curl and
   pilling.
4. Wear-test a full day; record hand feel, collar behaviour, odour.
5. Photograph against HIVOLT brand standards to confirm colour reproduces.
6. **Only then** score the matrix and select.

Skipping the wash test is how apparel brands discover shrinkage from customer
refunds instead of from a sample.
