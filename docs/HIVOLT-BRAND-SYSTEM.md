# HIVOLT-BRAND-SYSTEM.md — 2026-08-28

Brand: HIVOLT · Domain: hivolt-usa.com · Entity: Dn Global Trading LLC
(trading as HIVOLT).

## Positioning

Modern international fashion at high-volume commercial energy: premium
photography and typography doing the persuasion, not decorative luxury
color. Women-led today (catalog truth); men's direction staged. Mobile-native.

## Tone

Confident, concise, factual. No pseudo-French, no fake editorial words, no
invented designers, no fabricated claims. Prohibited unless verified:
"designed in USA", "made in USA", "sustainable", "organic", "premium
materials", any superlative ("#1", "trusted by X").

## Color system (V3, implemented in theme settings)

`#FFFFFF` ground · `#0A0A0B` ink · `#F7F7F5` soft · `#E5E5E5` line ·
`#6D6D6D` muted · `#D0021B` fashion red — sale/savings/cart-dot ONLY.
Conceptual balance: white ~65% / black ~20% / photo color ~10% / red ~5%.
Prohibited: beige-dominant UI, champagne/gold decoration, gradients, giant
rounded cards, SaaS chrome. Beige may exist inside photography only.

## Typography (implemented)

Host Grotesk 500 headings, 40px base, global uppercase; Inter 400 body
16px; nav 14px uppercase; buttons square, uppercase, 800 weight
(Impulse-native). Serif eliminated.

## Imagery system

Raw high-end commercial fashion photography only — no magazine/editorial
layout vocabulary in prompts, writing-free environments, unbranded
garments; women's: elevated-minimal winter knit styling; men's (dormant):
smart-casual minimal, same worlds and grade. Zero logos / zero generated
text — pixels decide, owner approves. AI = campaign/lifestyle slots only;
real supplier media = the SKU.

## UI principles

Full-bleed heroes and campaigns alternate with contained card systems;
tight vertical rhythm (mobile tighter); portrait product cards, 2-col
mobile / 4-col desktop; HTML-only overlay text; no dead links ever — a
destination without inventory does not get a link.

## Promotional rules

Only true claims (free US shipping, 60-day returns, no restocking fee).
Sale styling (#D0021B) appears only when a real discount exists. No
countdowns, no fake urgency, no fake badges.

## Logo

Text wordmark rendered by the theme today. A real vector wordmark is an
owner-supplied asset; generating one with AI typography is prohibited by
the zero-generated-text rule.
