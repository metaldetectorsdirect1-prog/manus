# HIVOLT — the five things that stand between you and a first sale

Everything reachable through the API is done. These five are admin-only.
Total time: about 20 minutes. Do them in this order — 1 and 2 are the ones
sitting directly in the checkout path.

Store admin: `https://admin.shopify.com/store/hivolt-3`

---

## 1. Point customer accounts at your own domain — 2 min

**Why:** `customerAccountsV2.url` is currently
**`https://account.focusfoxes.shop`** — a different brand. Every "Log in"
link, the *log in for faster checkout* prompt Shopify shows during checkout,
and the account link in every order confirmation email sends people there.

A buyer on `hivolt-usa.com` who watches `focusfoxes.shop` appear in the
address bar mid-checkout reads that as a redirect attack. This is the single
likeliest explanation for 24 checkout arrivals and zero completions.

**Do:** Settings → Customer accounts →
`https://admin.shopify.com/store/hivolt-3/settings/customer_accounts`
Set the account domain to `hivolt-usa.com`. If `focusfoxes.shop` is listed
under Settings → Domains and nothing else uses it, remove it.

**Verify:** the login URL shown on that settings page reads
`account.hivolt-usa.com`.

## 2. Replace the three legal policies — 5 min

**Why:** your Terms of Service currently says *"HIVOLT sells dietary
supplement products… not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any
disease."* The Refund Policy is about *"HIVOLT Collagen Peptides"*, *"opened
containers"* and *"original packaging"*. Both describe **automatic
subscription billing** you don't operate, and a 90-day window that
contradicts the 60 days on every product page.

**Shopify links the Refund Policy from the checkout page.** I have repointed
the site footer away from it, but the checkout link is generated by Shopify
and cannot be changed — only the policy content can.

**Do:** Settings → Policies →
`https://admin.shopify.com/store/hivolt-3/settings/legal`
Paste the replacements from **`audit/LEGAL-POLICY-REWRITE.md`** into Refund
policy, Terms of service and Contact information. Leave Shipping policy alone
— it is already correct.

Nothing in that file is newly drafted. Every term is lifted from your own
published returns pages, so the legal document and the storefront finally
agree. Have someone qualified read it before publishing.

**Decide first:** the store advertises two support addresses —
`support@hivolt-usa.com` on the customer-facing pages, and
`metaldetectorsdirect1@gmail.com` in the current policies. The replacements
use the first. **If that mailbox isn't monitored, every return request
vanishes.** Confirm it works or swap the address throughout.

## 3. Place one real order — 10 min

**Why:** no card has ever been charged by this store. 24 people reached
checkout and none paid, and I could not determine whether the payment gateway
actually works — `shopifyPaymentsAccount` is scope-blocked on this connector.
Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay all show as supported, which suggests
Shopify Payments *is* configured, but suggests is not the same as knows.

Until a card clears, every visitor you earn or buy is a coin flip.

**Do:** buy something from `hivolt-usa.com` with a real card, on a phone, not
logged in. Refund it afterwards if you want. It costs you the transaction fee.

**Watch for:** whether the checkout ever shows `focusfoxes.shop`, whether the
Refund Policy link at the bottom of checkout shows supplement text, and
whether the confirmation email looks like HIVOLT.

**Verify:** `ordersCount` moves off 0. My scheduled check will surface it.
