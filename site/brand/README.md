## Do not bake these into product images

Google Merchant Center prohibits promotional text, watermarks, logos and
borders on product images. Overlaying this mark on a PDP photo is a feed
disapproval risk, and the store's whole growth plan is Google Shopping.

Brand expression belongs in the theme — headers, frames, chrome, backgrounds —
not in the product image pixels. The product image should show the product,
cleanly, and nothing else.
