### The one decision still outstanding

No metal detector **retailer** account is anywhere near a million followers; every account at
that scale is a person. Without a recurring face on camera the realistic ceiling is tens of
thousands, not a million. Everything below works regardless — but it works far harder with a
face attached.

## 3. The loop

```
FILM (you)  →  EDIT  →  PUBLISH  →  LOG  →  READ  →  REALLOCATE
    ↑                                                      │
    └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Film** — ~14 field days across 60 days. One outing yields ~10 usable pieces. Desk pieces
(ASMR, Reacts) need no trip and cover bad weather.

**Edit** — CapCut or DaVinci Resolve, both free. Under 20 seconds. Burned-in captions.

**Publish** — Reels 2–4/day, plus the other surfaces in §5.

**Log** — every post in the tracker, 48 hours after publishing once Insights settles.

**Read** — the Format Scorecard applies the kill criterion automatically at five samples.

**Reallocate** — capacity moves off dead formats and onto proven ones from month 3.

## 5. Surfaces

Reels acquire strangers. Everything else keeps them. Instagram-only means you cannot afford to
leak the followers Reels bring in.

| Surface | Cadence | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Reels** | 2–4/day | The only surface that reaches strangers. The growth engine. |
| **Stories** | 3–5/day | Retention and engagement signals. Polls, quizzes, question boxes. |
| **Carousels** | 2/week | Saves. Instagram re-serves these to people who scrolled past. |
| **Lives** | 1/month | "Bring me your mystery finds" — heavy comment volume, costs an hour. |
| **Broadcast Channel** | 2–3/week | Direct line, no algorithm. Start past ~1,000 followers. |
| **Notes** | 2/week | One line, free, sits at the top of DMs. |
| **Collabs** | As they land | Borrowed audience, permanent on both grids. |

## 6. Measurement rules

- **5 posts minimum** per format before judging. One hit and one flop are both noise.
- **Follows per 1,000 reached** is the metric. Not views — a post can reach a million and
  convert nobody.
- **Under 50% 3-second retention** means the hook failed, not the format. Fix the hook, re-run.
- **Kill** any format below a third of your best rate after five samples, and reallocate.
- **Never delete a flop.** It is a data point.
- **Change one thing at a time** — format, hook style or length, never all three.

## 7. Per-post checklist

1. Burn in captions — most watch muted, and Instagram reads the transcript.
2. Say the US keywords out loud: *wheat penny, Mercury dime, junk silver, war nickel*.
3. Write custom alt text — a direct ranking factor, and almost nobody bothers.
4. Keyword in the caption's first line, written naturally.
5. Three to five hashtags. Not thirty.
6. Location-tag the real US place it was filmed.
7. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
8. Log it after 48 hours.

## 8. US targeting

Instagram has **no country setting** for organic reach. The mechanism is linguistic: it indexes
the Name field, bio, captions, on-screen text, spoken audio and alt text. Fill them with
vocabulary only Americans use.

**Use:** wheat penny · Mercury dime · Seated Liberty · Standing Liberty quarter · Indian Head ·
junk silver · pre-1965 · 90% silver · war nickel · clad · Civil War relic · minie ball ·
canister shot · Rev War · Colonial copper · large cent · old home site · tot lot

**Avoid:** hammered · milled · Roman · Celtic · Saxon · permission · ploughed field · PAS · FLO

## 9. Legal boundaries

Non-negotiable, and a Core compliance item.

- **Prohibited outright** on all National Parks, National Monuments and federal land under ARPA
  (1979). Filming a dig there risks prosecution *and* community reporting — which ends an
  account in a niche this small.
- **National Forests** — generally allowed in previously disturbed areas (campgrounds, picnic
  areas, swim beaches); prohibited in undisturbed areas. Varies by forest; check the ranger
  district.
- **BLM** — mostly allowed, ARPA still applies, no historical artefacts.
- **State parks** — varies. Permit, restricted, or banned.
- **Public beaches** — many coastal states allow it. **Florida is legal on many beaches with no
  permit** and is the best filming ground: legal, productive, and unmistakably American for
  location tagging.

Also standing: FTC disclosure on every seeded loan; written releases before publishing anyone
else's find or a ring recovery; music cleared for commercial use.
