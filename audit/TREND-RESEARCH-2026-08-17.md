## What changed on the store, all reversible

30 products reactivated (17 bras, 3 hoodies/sweatshirts, 2 polos, 5
leggings/yoga pants, 2 jackets, 1 pullover), zero failures, verified by count
read-back: **50 active**. The remaining 64 stay archived. Nothing was
deleted — deletion is irreversible, destroys specs and supplier images, and
buys nothing archive does not.

The sports-bras collection rule (title contains "Bra") now fills with 17
bras + 3 bra-containing sets — the organic-landing page is fully merchandised
again.

## The sourcing list, owner-gated

Knit sweaters/cardigans, satin slip dresses, and men's stretch chinos are the
three evidenced categories the supplier catalogue cannot honestly fulfil
today. Listing them without a supplier means taking money for goods that do
not exist. If Tapstitch (or an agent) carries equivalents, importing them
with real supplier photos and specs slots straight into the existing feed
pipeline.

## What was NOT done

No competitor images, titles, or copy were imported. TrendTrack surfaces
other stores' creative; copying it into listings is a copyright and
misrepresentation problem, and — per the owner's own adopted method — a
Merchant Center suspension class.
