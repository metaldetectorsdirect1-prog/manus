## How to read this
TrendTrack's `find_winning_products` returns *competitors'* products (their photos,
their pages) — it is a **demand signal**, never something to copy. We use it to decide
*what category sells*, then sell our own version with **Tapstitch's real product photos**
and **our own copy**. Copying a competitor's images/descriptions/reviews is the #1
Merchant-Center misrepresentation trigger — we do not do it.
