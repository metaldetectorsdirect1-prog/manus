# Checkout — what actually happens when someone tries to pay

Investigated 2026-08-09, because 24 sessions reached checkout in 40 days and
none completed. The theory being tested: something at the payment step is
broken, not the traffic.

## What is fine

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Payment provider | Shopify Payments live — Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay all enabled |
| US shipping rate exists | Yes. `FREE US Shipping (8-14 business days)`, $0, no conditions |
| US zone coverage | All 50 states, DC, PR, GU, VI, MP, AS, and military AA/AE/AP |
| Market | Only the **United States** market is ACTIVE. `International` is DRAFT |
| Variant weights | Real (0.18–0.20 kg), not zero |

The International zone in the General profile ($9.95) and the ~50 international
zones in the Tapstitch profile are **inert**: with the International market
disabled, Shopify will not let a non-US shopper check out at all. They are dead
configuration, not a leak, and were left alone.

## What was wrong

The catalogue does not live in the profile you would expect.

| Profile | Variants | Who owns it |
|---|---|---|
| General profile | 17 | us — the sets built in this repo |
| **Tapstitch: Special Line** | **500** | the supplier's app |

Every storefront surface promises free US shipping with no minimum — the
ticker on every page, the product page ship line, the cart, the shipping page
data sheet, the homepage FAQ, the shipping FAQ, and both FAQ JSON-LD blocks.

The Tapstitch profile's United States zone carried **89 active paid rates**
alongside the free one: `Special Line`, weight-banded in 100 g steps from
$3.40 (0–100 g) to $2750.54 (293–298 kg). A pair of leggings is 180 g, so a
one-item order matched the $4.20 band.

Free shipping was therefore never *unavailable* — the free rate is
unconditional and Shopify sorts cheapest first. But a shopper who has been told
"Free US shipping · no minimum" on every screen arrived at the shipping step
and was shown a priced option next to it. At the exact moment a first-time
buyer on an unknown brand is deciding whether the promises hold, the checkout
displayed a shipping charge. That is a doubt you cannot afford there.

## What changed

All 89 `Special Line` definitions in the Tapstitch **United States Zone** set
to `active: false`. The zone now offers exactly one option:

> FREE US Shipping (8-14 business days) — $0.00

Nothing outside the US zone was touched. The international zones keep their
rates, so if the International market is ever enabled the supplier's pricing is
still there.

One correction to my own work in the same session: I first added a second free
rate, `FREE Tracked Shipping (8–14 business days)`, before discovering the
existing `FREE US Shipping (8-14 business days)` at the end of the list — I had
only read the first 15 of 91 method definitions. Two identical $0 options is
worse than one, so the one I created was deleted.

### Reversal

Reactivate the 89 definitions in delivery zone
`gid://shopify/DeliveryZone/448778109160` (profile
`gid://shopify/DeliveryProfile/110455423208`, location group
`gid://shopify/DeliveryLocationGroup/111986082024`). Their IDs are consecutive:
`834373058792 + 32768n` for n = 0…88. None were deleted.

**This profile is app-managed.** If Tapstitch resyncs its rate card it may
reactivate them. Worth re-checking after any supplier price update.

## Still wrong, still owner-only

Two things at checkout that the API will not let this connector fix:

1. **The refund policy shown at checkout describes a supplement business.**
   "We stand behind the quality of HIVOLT Collagen Peptides", 90 days, customer
   pays return shipping, original packaging, subscription cancellations. The
   storefront says 60 days, free, worn and washed accepted. Shopify links this
   document from the checkout page. Replacement text is in `audit/policies/`.
   `shopPolicyUpdate` was retried this session and still returns
   `Access denied … Required access: write_legal_policies`.

2. **`customerAccountsV2.url` is `https://account.focusfoxes.shop`.** New
   customer accounts are on, so the "Log in" link at checkout sends a shopper
   from hivolt-usa.com to a different brand's domain. Settings → Customer
   accounts.

Both are read as evidence of a hijacked or resold storefront, and both sit on
the payment step. Until they are fixed, the shipping fix above cannot be
credited or blamed for what checkout does next.
