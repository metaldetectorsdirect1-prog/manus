# Eight-agent QA sweep of the live store

2026-08-12. Owner asked for 100 agents to visit the site and find what is wrong.
The storefront is blocked at this container's proxy — `hivolt-usa.com` and
`f36zps-yd.myshopify.com` both return **000**, and any agent spawned here shares
that proxy. A hundred browsers would have produced a hundred identical failures.

Eight agents audited the **real rendering path** instead: live theme Liquid read
through the Admin API, cross-referenced against live store data. That is how the
orphaned-template class of defect gets found at all — there is no URL to crawl.

## The two findings that corrected my own work

**The homepage never read the collection I reordered.** `templates/index.liquid`
does `collections['featured']`, keyed by **handle**. The collection titled
"Featured" had the handle **`frontpage`**. The lookup returned nil, the fallback
fired on every page load, and the homepage rendered `collections.all` sorted
`BEST_SELLING`. Thirteen products of hand-merchandising reached nobody, and the
lead tile was whatever was uploaded last — `mens-training-set-tee-shorts`, which
has no `spec.gsm`, on a page whose H1 is *"Every number on this page is
measured."*
→ **Fixed live** by renaming the handle to `featured`. No theme publish needed.

**The fabric index had zero inbound links.** `layout/theme.liquid` reads exactly
two linklists — `main-menu` and `footer`. The other four (`footer-shop`,
`footer-about`, `footer-help`, `footer-legal`, **24 links**) render nowhere. I
had put the fabric index in `footer-about` and reported it as linked.
→ **Fixed live**: `footer` rebuilt with 21 links across four columns.

## Fixed in `site/` — needs a theme publish

The worn-and-washed contradiction, closed at all seven remaining sites:
`product.liquid:195` (every product page), the returns data sheet, its visible
FAQ, its **FAQPage JSON-LD**, and `page.voltcore.liquid` twice.

The schema agent found one I had missed and it is the sharpest in the store:
the returns FAQPage told Google **the customer pays return postage**, while
`product.liquid:370` declares `"returnFees": "https://schema.org/FreeReturn"`
and the policy says free. **Two schema blocks on one domain making opposite
machine-readable claims.** The visible data sheet said "Paid by you" too — the
outlier against the policy *and* every product page. All now say free prepaid
label. JSON-LD revalidated: parses, six questions, mirrors the visible list.

## Verified clean — worth knowing

* **0 dead links** out of 125 audited across 7 menus and 60 hardcoded theme hrefs.
* **JSON-LD valid in every conditional branch** — Product tested at 192/192
  combinations, Organization 6/6, no trailing-comma failure anywhere.
* **Zero fabricated social proof.** No `aggregateRating`, no `Review`, no
  testimonials, no "X people bought", no countdown timers or viewer counts in
  the theme. The theme publishes the absence and emits it as JSON-LD.
* **0 broken HTML**, 0 duplicate SKUs, 0 fake discounts (all 17 compare-at
  prices genuinely above price), 0 zero-priced variants, 0 orphaned products.
* **All four bundle claims verify** against live prices: 38+54=92/79, 34+42=76/65,
  49+54=103/89, 38+59=97/79.
* **No previous-business content survives** in any published page, product
  description or live theme file.

## Open, ranked — not yet fixed

1. **7 more products advertise sizes they do not sell.** `men-s-jacquard-slim-mesh-t-shirt`
   claims S–4XL and does not sell **S** at all.
2. **`/pages/wholesale`** sells custom embroidery, trade pricing tiers and
   consolidated invoicing. None exist, and every set description says *"There is
   no logo printed on either piece."*
3. **`/pages/ambassadors`** promises **commission on every order** and personal
   discount codes. No affiliate infrastructure, no codes, zero orders.
4. **`/pages/track-order`** claims each garment is *"cut and sewn after you
   order it"*. TOS §1 says the opposite: *"We source manufacturer garments."*
5. **The 8–14 day delivery claim drops the 2–4 day dispatch step** on eight
   surfaces. True end-to-end is 10–18 business days. Fixed on the FAQ; the rest
   are theme files.
6. **96 of 113 product descriptions carry dropship superlatives** — "unmatched",
   "unparalleled", "premium", "guarantees maximum flexibility" — against the
   store's own published promise that *"if we cannot evidence a claim, we do not
   print it."* One set description says *"we publish them rather than describing
   the fabric as 'premium'"* while 40+ siblings describe the fabric as premium.
7. **Two incompatible fabric-weight band systems** are published. Under the
   Fabric Weight Index, 220 g/m² is "Mid — moves more than it holds"; the
   Voltcore page's entire thesis is that 220 holds shape. The brand's core
   differentiator is defined twice, differently.
8. **`page.contact.liquid` publishes "Phone — None"** while the Contact policy
   and every policy footer carry +1 914-650-2041.
9. **541/541 variants have no barcode/GTIN.**
10. **Two duplicate product listings** — `women-s-cropped-sports-bra` and
    `-1` are the same garment at the same $38 with different SKU roots.
11. **`settings.logo` is unset**, so Organization schema renders with no logo on
    every page. Theme setting, blocked from here.
12. **14 of 17 collections sort by `BEST_SELLING`** on a store with zero orders,
    which resolves to newest-created-first — arbitrary.

## Method note worth keeping

Three separate Shopify product query filters have now been caught lying in this
store: `available_for_sale`, `has_image`, and `metafields.mm-google-shopping.*`.
Every agent was warned, and the products agent confirmed 541/541 variants read
`availableForSale: true` while the filter claims all 113 are unavailable. **Read
the field back on the resource. Never conclude from a filter.**
