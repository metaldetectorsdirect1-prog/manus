HIVOLT Shipping Policy

Free standard shipping on every order. No minimum spend, no threshold to unlock.

Where we ship

The continental United States only. We do not ship internationally, and we do not ship to Alaska, Hawaii or US territories. If that changes it will be announced on our Shipping & Delivery page first.

Delivery window

8–14 business days from order to delivery. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

Orders are dispatched by a fulfilment partner in Illinois rather than from a warehouse we run ourselves, and that handling step is where the days go. We would rather quote a window we can hit than a shorter one we would miss on most orders.

Expedited shipping

Not available. There is one shipping method and it is free.

Tracking

Every order is tracked from dispatch. The tracking number is emailed to the address on the order, and it can take 24–48 hours to start updating after the first scan.

Delivery addresses we cannot use

  • PO boxes
  • Addresses outside the continental United States

APO and FPO addresses may take longer than the stated window.

Late or missing packages

If your order has not arrived within 14 business days of dispatch, email support@hivolt-usa.com with your order number. We open a trace with the carrier and either replace the order or refund it in full.

Questions

support@hivolt-usa.com — we reply within one business day.
