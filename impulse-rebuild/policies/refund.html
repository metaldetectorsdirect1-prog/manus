HIVOLT Return & Refund Policy

60-Day Guarantee

Every HIVOLT order is covered for 60 days from the delivery date. If a garment does not fit, does not hold up, or is simply not what you wanted, send it back for a full refund.

Return Window

60 days from delivery, on every order. Sale items are included.

Condition of Returned Items

  • Items must be unworn and returned with the original tags attached.
  • Trying a garment on is fine. Wearing it out, washing it, or removing the tags places it outside the window.
  • Original packaging is helpful but not required.

How to Start a Return

Email support@hivolt-usa.com with your order number and the item you are returning. Once your return is approved we send a prepaid return label within 2 business days.

Return Shipping

Free. We provide the return label and there is no restocking fee.

Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of your return reaching us. Depending on your bank, it can take a further 5-10 days to appear on your statement. Outbound shipping was free, so the full amount you paid is returned.

Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order for the size or color you want.

Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Items

Email support@hivolt-usa.com with photographs and we will ship a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer. This is in addition to the 60-day guarantee.

Order Cancellations

Email us as soon as possible after ordering. If your order has not yet been processed for dispatch, we will cancel and refund it in full.

Where We Ship and Accept Returns

HIVOLT sells and ships within the United States only. Returns are accepted from United States addresses only.
