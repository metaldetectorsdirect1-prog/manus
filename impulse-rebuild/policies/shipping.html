HIVOLT Shipping Policy

Free tracked shipping on every US order, no minimum.

Where We Ship

HIVOLT ships within the United States only.

Cost

Free on every order. There is no minimum spend and no surcharge at checkout. Shipping is shown as $0.00 before you pay.

Delivery Time

Orders are shipped by our fulfillment partners from their warehouses. Typical delivery estimates, based on our suppliers' stated shipping times: 2–6 business days for items stocked in US warehouses, and 10–15 business days for items stocked internationally. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

Tracking

Every order is tracked. Your tracking link is emailed once the carrier issues it, and you can follow your order on our Track Your Order page.

Wrong Address

Email support@hivolt-usa.com as soon as possible. If your order has not yet shipped, we will correct the address; once it has shipped, we will work with you on the best available fix.

Lost or Delayed Parcels

If your order is well past its estimated window, email support@hivolt-usa.com and we will investigate with the carrier and make it right.

Taxes and Duties

Prices are shown in US dollars. Any applicable sales tax is calculated and displayed at checkout.

Returns

Every order is covered by our 60-day return policy with free return shipping. See our Return and Refund Policy for details.
